Dragonfly: privacy

Dragonfly is a personal design tool used by one person, its author, Ana Gaina (ana-m-gaina). It is not offered to anyone else.

  • What it reads: the author's own Pinterest boards and pins, read-only, through the Pinterest API with her own login.
  • What it does with them: shows the pins as a mood board in an app that runs only on her own computer.
  • What it keeps: the login token, in a file on that computer. Pins and pictures are not stored, copied to a server or shared.
  • What it writes: nothing. It does not post, edit or delete anything on Pinterest.
  • Other people's data: none is collected.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/ana-m-gaina/dragonfly-privacy.
